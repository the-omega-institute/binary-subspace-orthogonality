# Certificate and search records

The historical `lean-verification.json` is preserved as reported. Its source
hashes match, but the recovered original log contains four native-evaluation
axioms omitted from the summary. The correction is
`lean-verification-audit-20260930.json`; see
[the audit](../notes/formal-verification-audit.md) for exact scope and repair.
This does not affect the independently checked
positive coloring certificates below.

The current full-graph theorem is supported by full-15-coloring.json and
full-15-check.json. The latter identifies the exact certificate and independent
checker hashes. checker-controls.json records rejection of three deliberately
invalid inputs: a missing vertex, a repeated subspace and a monochromatic edge.

The line-graph upper bound is supported by line-coloring.json and
line-coloring-check.json. Its lower bound 12 is the written weighted argument
in notes/line-coloring.md, independent of any SAT nonexistence report.

Search-15.json records the successful core search and full-graph extension.
The line-search and line-cover-search records include bounded exploratory
attempts. A status of unknown_time_limit is inconclusive. No unverified UNSAT
report is used in the current theorem statements. The direct eight-color
UNSAT proof was additionally checked with drat-trim revision
2e3b2dc0ecf938addbd779d42877b6ed69d9a985; the stronger written lower bound
supersedes that computation.

The first unfixed cover record identifies the earlier script hash used in that
experiment. The current script adds the justified fixed-six-class option; its
fixed experiment is line-cover-search-fixed.json. The full and fixed catalogs
are exploratory search inputs, not substitutes for a completeness proof.

The numerical experiment `refined-geometry.json`, reproduced by
`python3 develop/refine_geometry.py`, gives 30 groups, 29 multicolored.
It omits the actual H and K subspaces and is not a refinement of the 240
exact profiles. The earlier interpretation of a reduction from 139 to 29
in this README was incorrect.

`refined-geometry-arf.json` is reproduced by
`python3 develop/refine_geometry__arf.py` (two underscores in the filename).
It also gives 30 groups, 29 multicolored. Its self-orthogonal-vector parity
is a bilinear statistic, not an Arf invariant of a specified quadratic form.
The coauthor computations are preserved with corrected scope in the docstrings.

Run `python3 develop/audit_geometry_profiles.py` for a comparison retaining
the exact H and K. Its `geometry-profile-audit.json` gives 263 groups after
adding quotient dimension, 160 multicolored; adding alternating status makes
no further split. None of the original 139 multicolored groups is completely
resolved, and 2,288 vertices remain in multicolored groups. Group counts alone
are not an improvement metric: refinement can increase the multicolored count.
The report supplies member bases and colors for all 160 remaining exact groups.

The [geometric explanation](../notes/geometry-profile-audit.md) proves that
adjacent lines span(1) and span(2) share the exact profile and have isometric
rank-one quotients. No proper coloring can depend only on these data.

Generated CNF and DRAT files are not committed because no current result
requires a negative SAT certificate. They can be regenerated by the search
scripts, subject to solver time limits. Positive coloring verification needs
only the committed JSON and Python standard library.
