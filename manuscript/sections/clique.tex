\section{The exact clique number}
\begin{theorem}\label{thm:clique}
For every positive integer $n$,
\[
\omega(\OG_n^*)=\max\{n,N(\lfloor n/2\rfloor)+(n\bmod2)\}.
\]
\end{theorem}

The coordinate lines give the lower bound $n$.
For $r=\lfloor n/2\rfloor$, the subspace generated by
$e_1+e_2,\ldots,e_{2r-1}+e_{2r}$ is totally isotropic. Its nonzero subspaces
form a clique of size $N(r)$; when $n$ is odd, the line generated by $e_n$
can be added. These are the lower-bound constructions of~\cite{Nikandish}.

For the upper bound, let $\mathcal C$ be a clique and let $R$ be the sum of
the radicals of its members. Then $R\subseteq R^\perp$. Writing $r=\dim R$,
the images of the members not contained in $R$ form mutually orthogonal,
nonzero nondegenerate subspaces of $R^\perp/R$. They are linearly independent
as subspaces, so there are at most $n-2r$ of them. The members contained in
$R$ number at most $N(r)$. Thus
\[
 |\mathcal C|\leq N(r)+n-2r,\qquad 0\leq r\leq\lfloor n/2\rfloor.
\]
The successive increments of $N(r)$ are nondecreasing, so the maximum of
this expression occurs at an endpoint and gives Theorem~\ref{thm:clique}.
The complete radical/quotient proof is retained in the existing formal
module cited in Section~\ref{sec:verification}; this section is the integration
starting point for the expanded algebraic presentation.
