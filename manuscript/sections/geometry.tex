\section{Geometry of the precoloring}
\label{sec:geometry}
The color labels are the nonzero subspaces $W$ of the fixed totally isotropic
space $T$. The relation $W\subseteq H(U)$ determines exactly which fixed
clique colors are excluded at $U$. The additional invariant $U\cap T$
records how $U$ meets the isotropic space itself.

The reproducibility package groups the archived coloring by
$(\dim U,H(U),U\cap T)$. Outside the clique, $240$ such profiles occur,
of which $139$ receive more than one color in this certificate.
These data identify where a structural construction would need to refine
or reorganize the present choices. They do not exclude the existence of a
different coloring determined by a smaller collection of geometric invariants.
The files \texttt{results/coloring-geometry.json} and
\texttt{develop/analyze\_geometry.py} provide the complete counts and color labels.
